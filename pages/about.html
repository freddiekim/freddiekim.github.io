YOUNG FINANCE Management Philosophy| Company Introduction

Hello, I’m YOUNG, the CEO of the virtual one-person company, YOUNG FINANCE.
I created this page assuming I am the CEO of a virtual company.
Instead of simply running a blog, I plan to manage this page and update materials as if I were truly running a company.
I believe that investing is essentially a form of company management.
It may be small in scale and have no employees, but the principles are the same.
If my ability allows, I intend to organize and summarize my investments on a quarterly or annual basis.
Furthermore, I will not invest blindly, but will create an **Investment Template** to minimize failure.

* My Investment Philosophy *
I regard investing as building a fortress.
It’s like building and expanding a castle through "long-term investments (the castle)" and acquiring spoils of war by using "short-term investments (hiring soldiers)".
Hiring soldiers to seize spoils takes a relatively short time, but the risk is high. The soldiers might all be annihilated, or most might return to the castle defeated.
Therefore, I aim to hire farmers (using dividends and value stocks) to tend the land (investments) for stable growth. Farming only yields a harvest once a year (or three times a year if it's three-crop farming). This means the harvest period is long and requires a time of patience.
However, this approach provides a stable cash flow. This is the philosophy with which I intend to invest.
